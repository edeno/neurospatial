## Declared environments

The environment the driver analyses where the loader declares the apparatus (`geometry['environment']`), measured as above against the same samples. Verdict: misfit evidence (`_geometry.judge_declared`: a stretch of track whose samples lie to one side by more than a bin, a segment with no sample near it, a stated length off by more than 15 %), the stated parts and an independent reference. Beyond physical: the share of tracked samples farther than half the physical width from the track, a tracking measure (the tracked head swings past an unwalled track), not judged; in brackets the same share by speed, slowest band first (below the loader's running speed, up to 30 cm/s, above; bands in the details). Tolerance: the placement tolerance the track is built with (`track_width`), measured from the samples so about 1 % are dropped. Lateral: the median |lateral offset| and the worst stretch's median signed offset. Env / track: `own` when the reference is the geometry the track was declared from, which then measures only its binning.
